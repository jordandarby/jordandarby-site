The Client

Two friends working within the CPG industry saw firsthand how dependent the United States had become on imported raw materials and outsourced manufacturing when the COVID-19 pandemic disrupted supply chains. They realized most Americans were using products requiring materials imported from thousands of miles away, and recognized the damage this caused to the environment, domestic jobs, and national sovereignty. It didn’t sit right with them, and they wanted to be part of the solution.

The Challenge

Maverick’s knew they needed to educate consumers so they’d demand domestic sourcing and manufacturing. I partnered with Maverick’s to bring their bold ideas to life. They had a great product and mission but needed a brand. Facing market incumbents and a target market that would be hesitant to change their products and routines, it was crucial that Maverick’s stood out on the shelf, educated the consumer, and earned trust.

The Solution

I aimed to produce a brand that would feel familiar to the category leaders while still feeling new and authentic. I accomplished this by utilizing a minimal graphic system, a handwritten logo, and a down-to-earth, patriotic color palette. The packaging is a bold patriotic red, which creates a vibrant color block on the shelf that grabs attention. This deep red pairs wonderfully with the off-white logo, making it possible to see the packaging from far away. Additionally, the nature-inspired pattern allows omnichannel shoppers to effortlessly identify the scent. The information on the front of the packaging is purposely restricted to the most important things: the logo, the product, and the USP.

“The brand’s logo, off-white and hand-drawn, is complimented by a sharp and patriotic color palette of red and navy.”

“It was important that the new packaging stood out on the shelf, educated the consumer, and earned trust.”

The Outcome

The result is a brand that can sit beside category leaders and still stand apart: a bold red shelf block, a hand-drawn off-white logo, and packaging that communicates the mission in seconds — with a name built around being the first men’s grooming brand that is 100% American-sourced and manufactured.
