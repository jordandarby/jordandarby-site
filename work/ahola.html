Ahola

A calm, cool, and collected brand identity inspired by surfing.

The Client

Ahola is a surfing retailer in San Diego, CA that carries surfboards, menswear, and surfing accessories such as boardshorts and sunglasses. Additionally, they sell branded merchandise.

The Challenge

Ahola needed a brand refresh that matched the vibe of their store. They wanted something sharp yet laidback. I worked with them to make it happen.

The Solution

Inspired by the laidback nature of beach life and surfing, I designed a brand that was minimal and clean but still retained some sun-bleached soul. The brand feels like you’re hanging out beachside.

“The relaxed but calculated look and feel captures both the mood of the beach and the strategic precision required to surf skillfully.”

The Outcome

The brand identity positions Ahola as both a retail destination and a lifestyle brand that transports the feeling of surfing and sunny days.
