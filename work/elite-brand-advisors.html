Elite Brand Advisors

An elite design studio needs an elite brand identity.

Elite Brand Advisors

Design

The Client

Elite Brand Advisors is a design studio that produces stylized motion graphic content and brand creative for CPG brands.

The Challenge

Elite Brand Advisors needed a brand identity that would differentiate them from a crowded field of design studios and social media agencies, and earn trust with the CPG brands they wanted to win — anchored around a lion as the central mark.

The Solution

I aimed to produce a brand identity that was modern and appropriate for a B2B business that was also fun. The logo is a lion with a stoic and confident expression. It is symmetrical and features an iconic geometric mane.

“The website is straightforward and on-brand, featuring a gallery of looping motion graphic samples.”

The Outcome

The identity distinguishes the company from other design studios and social media agencies while still coming across as professional and trustworthy.
