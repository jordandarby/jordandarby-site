Minimally bold branding inspired by letterpress typography.

The Client

Boilermaker’s Son is a bottled cocktail made in Kansas City using rock candy, Florida navel oranges, raw honey, and aged bourbon whiskey. Inspired by the generation of blue-collar workers that built Kansas City, the brand stands for hard, honest work and celebrating a job well done.

The Challenge

I partnered with Boilermaker’s Son pre-launch to bring their brand to life. Our discussions uncovered a yearning for a modern brand identity inspired by the past that honored their devotion to quality and craftsmanship without the gimmicks.

The Solution

Boilermaker’s Son’s branding pays homage to the past by taking cues from vintage letterpress compositions. The new identity deploys minimal graphic elements — the logo and a decisive color palette — that reflect the purity of the ingredients and the brand’s devotion to the concept of “no gimmicks”. The brand identity features a distinct typographic system that can be applied to a variety of brand assets, ensuring brand consistency.

“I set out to capture the essence of blue-collar grit with a brand identity that’s eye-catching and cool.”

“Rejecting the snobbery that often surrounds premium drinks brands, Boilermaker’s Son embodies the concept of ‘humble quality’.”

“The new identity positions Boilermaker’s Son as a bottled cocktail that advocates for hard work, craftsmanship, and worthwhile celebrations.”
