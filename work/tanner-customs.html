Tanner Customs

A vintage-Americana badge for a father-and-son custom motorcycle shop.

Client
Tanner Customs
Deliverables
  • Branding

Role
Creative Direction
Design

The Client

Tanner Customs is a father-and-son-owned custom motorcycle shop that converts base motorcycles into something unique.

The Challenge

After years of making well-designed, custom motorcycles, the Tanners felt it was time for a well-designed, custom brand identity for themselves. They envisioned something minimal and masculine that’d look great on a t-shirt.

The Solution

Walking around Tanner Customs’ shop, I saw tons of vintage Americana — think hand-painted signs and old-school posters. Talking with them, they were stoked about the idea of a logo that took inspiration from the vintage things they collected, with a modern twist. That’s what I created, and it does look great on a t-shirt.

“I researched vintage American badges and created an original wordmark emblem that stylistically harkens back to those early days of branding.”
