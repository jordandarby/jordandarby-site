Questions

Do I need to be home?

No. We just need to reach the pool, and we text you when we're done.

What if the gate is locked?

Give us the code, or tell us where the key is. We latch it behind us every time.

Do you look after second homes?

Yes. For lake homes and second homes we check the pool, equipment and gate every visit, and send photos you can see from anywhere.

What if it storms?

Rain doesn't stop us. Lightning does, and we come back the next day at no charge.

Do you open and close pools?

Yes. Openings in March and April, closings in October and November, priced by pool size.

Do you service hot tubs?

Yes, with the pool for $15 a visit, or on their own through the winter.

Text your address and a photo of the pool. We'll reply with a quote, or a time to walk the property with you.
