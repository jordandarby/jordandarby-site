FAQ

Before You Reach Out

The things most people want to know first

These are general guidelines. Every engagement is different, so scope, timing, and terms are confirmed in writing before any project begins.

How many rounds of revisions do I get?

Two rounds on Brand Starter and Packaging & Label, three on Full Brand Identity. Each round is a consolidated set of feedback from your side, which keeps things moving and keeps the scope steady. Extra rounds are available at an hourly rate if you want them.

What do you need from me to start?

A short conversation about your business, your customers, and what you’re trying to achieve — plus any existing assets, product details, or references you like. If you have print specs or a manufacturer already, bring those too. I’ll send a short questionnaire so nothing gets missed.

How do payments work?

50% to reserve your spot on the calendar, 50% on final delivery. Invoices are sent electronically and I’ll always confirm scope and price in writing before any work begins — no surprises.

What if I don’t like the first direction?

That’s what the revision rounds are for. I present concepts with the reasoning behind them, so feedback is a conversation about strategy rather than taste. In practice, aligning on direction before the detail work is what prevents this from happening.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope and how quickly feedback comes back. Once we’ve talked through what you need, I’ll give you a schedule in writing before any work starts — and I’ll tell you upfront if your deadline isn’t realistic.

Do you work with businesses outside Northwest Arkansas?

Yes. I’m based in Northwest Arkansas and enjoy working with local businesses, but most of the process happens over video calls and email, so location isn’t a constraint.

Do you handle printing and production?

I deliver print-ready artwork built to your printer or manufacturer’s specifications. You or your supplier provide the dieline; I make sure the artwork is production-correct and coordinate with them on files, colors, and proofs. You contract the printer directly — I make sure they get what they need.
